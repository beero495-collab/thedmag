OVERVIEW

DMAG ("we", "us", or "our") operates as a digital product discovery and recommendation platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Short version: We collect only what we need. We don't sell your data. We use your email to send relevant product updates — nothing else.

02

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect the following types of information:

  • Email address — when you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter or weekly picks.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on page, clicks, and device/browser type via analytics tools (Google Analytics 4 / Microsoft Clarity).
  • Referral data — the source or campaign that brought you to DMAG, used to understand which content performs best.

We do not collect names, phone numbers, payment information, or any sensitive personal data.

03

HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Send you curated product recommendations and weekly picks (email subscribers only).
  • Understand which content and products resonate with our audience.
  • Improve the website experience and fix issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

We will never use your email for unsolicited promotions, and we will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

04

COOKIES & ANALYTICS

This website uses cookies and third-party analytics tools to understand visitor behavior:

  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymized traffic and behavior data.
  • Microsoft Clarity — session recordings and heatmaps to improve UX.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Opting out of analytics will not affect your ability to use the site.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of every site you visit.

06

YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address listed on our Contact page.
