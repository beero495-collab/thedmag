WE'LL KEEP IT STRAIGHT

Our commitment

DMAG earns commissions when you buy products through our links. This never changes which products we feature. We only recommend tools we believe are genuinely useful — whether or not they pay us anything.

This disclosure exists because we believe you deserve to know exactly how this site works. No fine print, no buried footnotes.

01

HOW AFFILIATE LINKS WORK

When you click a product link on DMAG and complete a purchase on the product's website, we receive a commission from the product creator or affiliate network. This is a standard industry practice used by review sites, newsletters, and recommendation platforms worldwide.

Here's what you need to know:

  • Affiliate commissions are paid by the product vendor — not by you. Your price is exactly the same whether you use our link or not.
  • We receive no advance payment, guaranteed placement, or editorial control from product creators in exchange for featuring their tools.
  • Our editorial decisions are made independently, based on product quality and relevance to our audience.

03

OUR CURATION STANDARDS

DMAG was built on a simple premise: there's too much noise in the digital tools space, and people deserve honest recommendations. Our curation process follows these principles:

  • We only feature products we have personally evaluated or have strong third-party evidence for their value.
  • We don't feature products simply because they offer high commissions.
  • We remove products from the site if they no longer meet our quality standards, regardless of any affiliate relationship.
  • We clearly label product types (AI Tool, Course, SaaS Platform) so you know what you're getting before you click.

Our test: Would we recommend this product to a friend even if there was no commission involved? If the answer is no, it doesn't go on the site.

04

FTC COMPLIANCE

This disclosure complies with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and testimonials, which require that material connections between endorsers and product creators be clearly disclosed.

As an internationally accessible platform, DMAG also aims to meet the transparency standards recommended by advertising regulators in the UK (ASA/CAP), EU, and other jurisdictions.
