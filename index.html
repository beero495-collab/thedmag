What is DMAG and how does it work?
+

DMAG is a curated digital product discovery platform. We research and hand-pick AI tools, marketing systems, and digital business resources — then present them in one clean place. We earn a small affiliate commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you.

How do you choose which products to feature?
+

Simple filter: would we actually use this ourselves? If yes, and if it's relevant to creators or entrepreneurs building online — it makes the list. Commission size is not the deciding factor.

What's in the weekly email?
+

One email per week. One featured tool or resource, a short honest take on why it matters. No promotional filler. Unsubscribe with one click anytime.

Are these products beginner-friendly?
+

Most are, yes. The descriptions tell you who each product is actually for. We try to be specific so you don't waste time on something that isn't right for your level.

Is there any cost to browse DMAG?
+

None. DMAG is completely free to browse. We earn affiliate commissions when you buy through our links. No paywalls, no subscriptions required from you.
